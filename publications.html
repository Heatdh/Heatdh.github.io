NeurIPS 2026 · Main track · First author

PhysDNet: Physics-Driven Gradient Amplification for Real-Time Image Dehazing

Rayen Dhahri, Lei Sun, Victor Javier Kartsch Morinigo, Alice Pagnoux, Luca Benini, Danda Pani Paudel, Luc Van Gool

A lightweight network fuses time-gated NIR imagery with sparse LiDAR. A physics-based auxiliary head amplifies gradients in regions of dense haze during training; only the direct restoration head is deployed.

The contribution

Use physical inversion to shape optimization, concentrating learning on degraded pixels while keeping the inference footprint unchanged.

02 2025

EurIPS 2025 workshop · First author

Quant-Trim in Practice: Improved Cross-Platform Low-Bit Deployment on Edge NPUs

Rayen Dhahri, Steffen Urban · Carl Zeiss AG

Workshop: Rethinking AI: Efficiency, Frugality, and Sustainability

Different edge compilers can turn the same floating-point checkpoint into very different low-bit models. Quant-Trim combines progressive fake quantization with reverse pruning to reduce sensitivity to scaling and clipping choices.

The contribution

A training-time approach to hardware-neutral checkpoints, designed to reduce the need for backend-specific retraining and graph changes.

NeurIPS 2024 · Main track · First author

Shaving Weights with Occam’s Razor: Bayesian Sparsification for Neural Networks Using the Marginal Likelihood

Rayen Dhahri, Alexander Immer, Betrand Charpentier, Stephan Günnemann, Vincent Fortuin

Sparsifiability via the Marginal likelihood (SpaM) uses Bayesian model selection and sparsity-inducing priors to train networks that are easier to prune. The approach supports structured and unstructured sparsification.

The contribution

Improve the model’s capacity to be pruned during training. Reuse the Laplace posterior precision to obtain Optimal Posterior Damage, an inexpensive pruning criterion.
